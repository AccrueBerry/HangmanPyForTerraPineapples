Type a letter into the terminal and press enter to guess a letter, once you have all the letters or have a guess for the full word, type it in to guess! \n*****BE SURE TO INCLUDE ALL PUNCTUATIONS, SPACES, etc. NO UPPERCASE LETTERS!!*****\nAfter you have less than 6 lives, you are allowed to exchange a life for a hint by typing \"hint\"! Be careful though, as helpful as some hits can be, many hints are more obscure references and may seem unrelated if you don't catch the reference. Along with hints there are two other related resources: free hints that allow you to use a hint without using a life, though you cannot use it before losing 2 lives a usual or without the other resource item, quick hints that allow you to use a hint before you have lost two lives. free hints are obtained when you are able to guess the word before losing 2 lives and you are given a quick hint every 5 rounds. \nThere is a mechanic called \"stars\" in this version, requiring you to guess the word without using more than 2 lives. This means you cannot get the star if you need a hint, as the hint will put you at 4 lives, making that round inelligible for the star. there is also a streak counter that counts your consecutive stars.\n there are secret messages at certain checkpoints, 5 score, 10 score, so on. there is also messages associated with stars and most importantly star streaks, which are more or less frequent, be careful and hit those goals!
